Demo Salon offers three approved services. A haircut costs $35 and takes about 30 minutes. A beard trim costs $18 and takes about 20 minutes. A haircut and beard trim costs $45 and takes about 45 minutes. All listed services are active in the service catalog. These prices and durations come from the canonical demo salon fixture.
